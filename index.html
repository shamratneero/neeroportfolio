01 / MEDICAL AI2026 · MICAD ACCEPTED

A high AUC is only
part of the story.

A solo-authored chest X-ray triage study evaluating four deep learning models on 112K+ NIH ChestX-ray14 images, with external validation on VinDr-CXR.

Examining disagreement between ranking, localization, and calibration.
PyTorchDenseNet-121ConvNeXt-TinyFastAPI
Read the study overview ↗

Evaluation

Compared ranking performance, localization, and calibration to assess whether apparent model quality translates into useful behavior for resource-constrained triage.

Reported findings

Cardiomegaly localization IoU reached up to 0.47. Per-class isotonic calibration yielded approximately 40-fold lower expected calibration error than global temperature scaling in the study’s evaluation.

Engineering

Built a FastAPI and Streamlit triage prototype and profiled CPU/GPU latency and memory. The prototype supports deployment-aware evaluation in resource-constrained settings.

Status

Accepted at MICAD 2026. See the publication entry below for the full title.

02 / MULTIMODAL LEARNING2026 · SUBMITTED

Auditing Alzheimer’s
classification models.

Evaluated clinical, imaging, and MRI–genetics fusion models using subject-level cross-validation on OASIS-3 and external evaluation on ADNI.

External fusion AUC: 0.6330 → 0.8014 with validation Brier score checkpoint selection.
PyTorch3D CNNOASIS-3ADNI
Read the study overview ↗

Study design

Five-fold subject-level cross-validation on 365 OASIS-3 subjects, followed by external evaluation on 1,287 ADNI participants.

The audit

Investigated discrepancies between mean fold-wise and pooled out-of-fold AUC. Identified degenerate AUC-selected checkpoints and cross-fold probability incoherence.

Reported result

Selecting checkpoints by validation Brier score improved external fusion AUC from 0.6330 to 0.8014 in this cross-cohort evaluation.

Status

Manuscript submitted to Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics (CMIG), 2026.

03 / LONGITUDINAL NEUROIMAGING2026 · ONGOING

Following progression.
Accounting for uncertainty.

An ADNI longitudinal cohort study with explicit 24-month outcome rules, developing MRI-derived and clinical predictors of MCI progression.

954 baseline T1 MRI studies selected for imaging processing.
Structural T1 MRIADNIFastSurfer
Explore the ongoing work ↗

Cohort definition

Defined 1,744 baseline MCI participants: 196 converters, 829 stable MCI, and 719 right-censored, using explicit 24-month outcome rules.

Current scope

Selected 954 baseline T1 MRI studies for processing and am developing MRI-derived and clinical predictor pipelines. Imaging analysis and progression modeling are ongoing; predictive results are not yet reported.
